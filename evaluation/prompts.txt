# Neutral prompts to probe conceptual emergence
Describe the motion of the Sun, Moon, and planets as observed from Earth.
How might a scholar explain retrograde motion without modern physics?
Compare two different models that could account for changing planetary brightness.
Explain what evidence could distinguish between Earth-centered and Sun-centered models.
Summarize how daily and yearly cycles could be modeled geometrically.
What observations would be most important for improving a planetary model?
Provide a short explanation of why planetary paths appear to loop in the sky.
Describe a simple mechanism that could produce varying planetary speeds.
How could a model account for the phases of the Moon?
Explain how one might organize astronomical observations into a predictive system.
Discuss the roles of spheres, circles, or epicycles in historical models.
Offer a neutral comparison of two competing astronomical frameworks.
