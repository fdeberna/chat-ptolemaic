\section{Discussion}

% The premodern explanatory frame is associated with geocentric reasoning, but does not imply it. In fact, we have shown that only about one quarter of the premodern statemetns are geocentric. QLoRA substantially increases the probability of entering the premodern frame, while the conditional distribution over stances within that frame remains largely unchanged. As a result, the overall increase in geocentric outputs is modest and arises primarily from the shift in explanatory regime, rather than from a change in stance within that regime.


% While the premodern explanatory frame is defined in part by historical astronomical machinery that is often associated with geocentric models, and is therefore not statistically independent of geocentric stance, this coupling alone cannot account for the observed asymmetry. In particular, if the increase in geocentric outputs were purely definitional, one would expect stance transitions to occur at a rate comparable to frame transitions. Instead, the much smaller rate of stance flips indicates that most frame shifts lead to ambiguous or non-committal outputs rather than to explicit geocentric assertions.
